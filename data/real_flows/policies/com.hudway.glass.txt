PRIVACY POLICY

Hudway, LLC and its affiliate companies (collectively "Hudway", "us", or "we") appreciate the interest you have shown in our products and services. This Privacy Policy outlines our guidelines for the information Hudway collects when you interact with Hudway, such as when you visit our Websites – www.hudwayapp.com and www.hudwayglass.com (collectively "Websites"), when you use our smart phone applications (all our apps collectively "the Apps"), when you subscribe to our newsletter or when you contact our Customer Support.

The Websites and the Apps are not intended for use by persons under the age of 16. If you are under 16, you may not download or use the Apps.

"Personal Information" is information about you that is personally identifiable to you, such as your name, address, e-mail address, or phone number.

"Anonymous Information" means information that is not associated with or linked to your Personal Information. Anonymous Information does not permit identification of you. Anonymous Information, such as basic usage statistics regarding your use of the products and services offered by Hudway, does not by itself identify you to Hudway, though such information may be unique or consist of or contain information that you consider personal.

In accessing our Websites and using our Apps, you accept all the terms and conditions of this Privacy Policy. You also agree that Hudway may amend and/or revise this Privacy Policy at its discretion, and you agree to be bound by these revisions and amendments. Your Personal Information may be processed in the country where it was collected as well as in other countries (including the United States) where laws regarding processing of Personal Information may be less stringent than the laws in your country. If you do not agree with the terms and conditions of this Privacy Policy we request that you please immediately cease using and accessing the Websites as well as the Apps and services for which you need to provide Personal Information.

Collection of Information by Hudway

The Personal Information we gather from you helps us learn about our visitors and customers. We use this information to provide you with our products and services, to develop new products and services, to better tailor the features, performance and support of our products and services, and to offer you additional information and opportunities.

We collect Personal Information that you submit to us voluntarily through our Websites and your use of our products and services. We may collect Anonymous Information passively or actively. The types of Personal Information and Anonymous Information we collect are:

1. If you set up an account while using the Apps, we will collect your contact details such as email address as well as other information you provide. Please note that you are not obligated to create an account to use our Apps, however, some features may only be available to users who create an account.

2. If you buy products or services from our Websites, we will collect your full name, the name of your company (if applicable), your postal address, e-mail, and phone number.

3. If you request customer support services for our products and/or services, we will collect Personal Information to enable us to provide customer support services to you, including your name, email address, and other information as applicable. We may also collect other information about Hudway products you own or Apps you use, such as the serial numbers, date of purchase, order details, and information related to a support or service issue, some of which might include Personal Information.

4. If you subscribe to Hudway navigation-related Apps and set up an account, Hudway may collect location data from your device as well as the history of your completed trips while using the Apps.

5. Hudway may collect Personal Information through surveys for market research purposes to gain a better understanding of our customers, to improve our products and services, and to develop new products and services. The information we collect in such instances depends on the survey, but might include your name, address, age, information about your use of our services and products and the like.

Cookie Policy

Our Websites use cookies to allow Hudway to collect data about visitors of the Websites that we may associate with Personal Information that we have collected from you. We do it to customize and personalize our Websites for their visitors. A "cookie" is a small text file that is placed on your hard disk by a web server and is used by our Websites to identify your computer whenever you visit our Websites. Hudway uses cookies to store your preferences and other related information. Hudway does not use cookies to gather other information from your personal computer. Cookies can be removed by following Internet browser help file directions. If you choose to disable cookies, some areas of the Websites may not look or work properly.

Information Collected Via Technology

As you navigate our Websites, certain Anonymous Information may also be collected passively, including your Internet protocol address, browser type, and operating system. We also use cookies and data like Uniform Resource Locators (URL) to gather information regarding the date and time of your visit and the information which you searched and viewed. We use this Anonymous Information to measure the traffic on our Websites and to help us make our Websites more useful. We delete this information after a period of time.

Meta-data that we collect

Hudway collects information about the use of the Apps. For example, Hudway may record the frequency and scope of your use of the Apps, the duration of your sessions, information about the device on which you have installed the Apps, advertisements that you view or click on, your communications with other users and third parties, and the geographic location of the computer system that you are using to log-in.

Using Your Personal Information

Personal Information will be collected, processed, and used by Hudway for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

Your Personal Information may be used by Hudway in several ways. First, we collect it to perform our obligations resulting from the delivery of our products and / or Apps to you.
